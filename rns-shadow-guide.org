- {{{N1}}}, {{{O1}}}, {{{S1}}}, {{{R1}}}, {{{E1}}}, {{{G1}}} :: _Normal_ and _gem_ variants of Primary.
- {{{R1}}}, {{{R2}}}, {{{R3}}}, {{{R4}}} :: _Ruby_ variants of each respectively: Primary, Secondary, Special and Defensive.
- $2' \ggg 3 > 1 > 4 > 2$ :: move priority for {{{R2}}}
  + {{{secl}}} ($2'$) is drastically favoured and clicked whenever available;
  + Followed by Special, Primary, Defensive and (non-leap) Secondary.
- AoE :: Area-of-Effect; typically used in context of hitting multiple enemies, e.g. fights in Churchouse or Sanctum.
- oGCD :: Shadow's S3, S4; these can be used without sacrificing GCD time spent on other
- prio :: priority; typically what item/gems should be picked and by whom
  + BiS :: Best in Slot; universal best upgrade.
  + line :: if buying a gem, typically how broad or strong its item-gem synergies are.
- slam :: a forced gem-item pick to solve the immediate stage/encounter.
  + In R&S, often this does expand your item lines off base, even if not BiS.

* REVIEW Class Summary
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h:class_summary
:END:

Shadow is a mobile close-quarters bunny with a potent versatility; she excels when playing flexibly, due to {{{Shackles}}}.
Many of her effects are unique or inspired from other classes and effects.

Shadow is best characterized by having:
- Decent-damage and short GCD (1.0s) abilities.
  + Close-Range multi-hit {{{abl(N1)}}} *Primary* and {{{abl(N2)}}} *Secondary* abilities.
    + No slows on these abilities.
  + {{{abl(N2)}}} *Secondary* having a leap, similar to Heavyblade's (HB) Special.
  + {{{abl(N3)}}} *Special* that hits all enemies on short CD.
  + {{{abl(N4)}}} *Defensives* with damage and GCD.
- Shares the fastest base movement speed (m/s) with Assassin (Sin).
- {{{Shackles}}} status effect.

#+caption: Class Advantages Table
| Pros                                   | Cons                                        |
|----------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------|
| Unique, gacha-like effects             | Defensive has GCD                           |
| Higher APM                             | Complex rotations ({{{Shackles}}})                |
| Secondary invulnerability              | Punishes bad targetting and leaps           |
| Global+4P damage multipliers ({{{O2s}}} {{{G3s}}}) | Weak Special-only builds                    |
| Consistent damage and AoE solves       | Almost 0 hacky Rabbitluck 8k+ damage builds |

** REVIEW General Gameplan
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h:gameplan
:END:

Shadow typically should be playing up close.
In more optimal play with {{{R1}}} / {{{R2}}} / {{{R4}}},
you should be playing just under 3 Rabbitleaps, then moving out enable leap when you need it.

In MP, Shadow adjusts by walking for teammates as she shares _fastest base speed with Assassin_ and, like Sin,
has no slows on two of three attacks.

*** REVIEW Multiplayer Item Conflicts

Most item conflicts do and _should occur with Sin, Dancer, SS and Druid._
- 1P: Shadow build towards Primary, Secondary builds, multihit or low GCD.
- 4P: Shadow omits Primary typically, but same pattern.

Most of the time on Shadow, your damage will come from abilities, so you often want a mix of (non-exhaustive):

1. High base damage abilities ({{{R1s}}}, {{{R2s}}}, {{{O3s}}})
2. Autocast abilities and oGCD ({{{R4s}}} \ggg {{{S1s}}}, {{{G1s}}}, {{{S3s}}})
3. Additive or percent damage ({{{i(Pocketwatch)}}}, {{{i(Shadow-Bracelet)}}})
   + Additionally, Bleed and sap effects ({{{i(Leech-Staff)}}}, {{{i(Blood-Vial)}}})
4. Curse ({{{O2s}}}, {{{i(Curse-Talon)}}}, {{{i(Darkmagic-Blade)}}})
5. Buffs ({{{G3}}}, {{{i(Butterfly-Hairpin)}}})
   + Multihit focus ({{{O4s}}},  {{{i(Crane-Katana)}}} / {{{i(Falconfeather-Dagger)}}})
   + Large base focus ({{{i(Beach-Sandals)}}} / {{{i(Flamedancer-Dagger)}}})
6. Critical hit items ({{{i(Pointed-Ring)}}})

This is independent of any Magicklock trinket effects, cooldowns or item effects (e.g. {{{Lefthand-Cast}}}.)

** REVIEW Inspirations from other Classes and Loot
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h:inspirations

Shadow gains some distinct effects that appear in other classes, namely:
- {{{S2}}}'s range and charge effect like Dancer's Secondary.
- {{{G3}}} granting the Shadowcry buff, similar to Dancer's Defensive and better uptime.
- {{{O2}}} granting Shadowcurse debuff, similar to Spellsword's (SS) Opal Defensive and better uptime.
- {{{G1}}} granting 100 damage 25% of the time to all enemies, is practically a {{{Brightstorm-Spear}}} and {{{Darkstorm-Knife}}}.

** REVIEW Gacha ability
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h:gacha
:END:

{{{E3}}} grants Shadow common access to, often loot-only or class-exclusive, effects like Berserk, Sap and Blackstrike.
This distinctly allows Shadow to build into debuffs, multi-hit (on Special); or support most Shadow builds.
- See [[https://rns.miraheze.org/wiki/Shadow][Shadow (rns.miraheze.org)]] for exhaustive list of effects.

** REVIEW Practices on Targetting
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h:targetting
:END:
#+MACRO: nt @@html: <span style="font-size: small; border:2px DarkSalmon solid; padding: 1px;">@@Nearest Target@@html:</span>@@
#+MACRO: ct @@html: <span style="font-size: small; border:2px DarkSeaGreen solid; padding: 1px;">@@Change Target@@html:</span>@@

Targetting goes based on *enemy ID order*. Once you determine it for e.g. Tassha, it's typically fixed for that pattern.
# need to verify this.

Regarding player controls {{{nt}}} and {{{ct}}} (ignoring shielded enemies.)
- {{{nt}}} :: changes enemy target to _nearest enemy_.
- {{{ct}}} :: /two functions:/
  a. While held, targets _center between enemies_ and slows your character.
  b. When released, _advances targetting_ in queue.
- {{{nt}}} press \land {{{ct}}} released :: _resets to frontmost enemy_ in queue.

#+begin_quote
[Advanced] For reset to frontmost enemy in queue, as long as a "new" {{{nt}}} starts or ends during {{{ct}}}, releasing {{{ct}}} will reset targetting.

Let's say you held this {{{nt}}} past the target reset, it is now consumed.
Holding a consumed held {{{nt}}} like this will not affect proceeding {{{ct}}}'s,
such that further target resets require {{{nt}}} to be refreshed (released and reclicked.)
#+end_quote

*** Untargettables

:CUSTOM_ID: h:untarget

Some enemy status effects are untargettable (non-exhaustive):

1. Certain Butterflies with Bubble Shields spawned in *Subterra Sanctum*, as well:
   - Plee's butterflies may be shielded in *Geode*.
   - Eternal Bloom herself during ads spawns.

*** Tips: Avoiding Leap
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h:avoid_leap
:END:

If you're in a fight with multiple enemies, and you do _not want to leap_:
- Identify your current target (and if they're low health).
- Pressing {{{nt}}} prior to {{{sec}}}.
- If using {{{ct}}} to slow down, pressing {{{nt}}} afterwards.
- If enemies are opposite of each other (e.g. Meg and Varo in Churchmouse), you can position center and hold {{{ct}}}.

#+begin_quote
Vice versa, I may refresh and force leap by pressing {{{nt}}} \rightarrow {{{ct}}} (or {{{nt}}} + {{{ct}}}) rather than scrolling the targets.
#+end_quote

*** Tips: Alternate forms of movement
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h:alt_movement
:END:

- Alternatively, use "tapping" movement keys (*streaming*) for micromovements.
- Using {{{spe}}} or {{{def}}} to slow movement temporarily.
  
** REVIEW Invuln Chaining
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h:leap_chain
:END:

For damage/consistency, see [[*Ruby Leap Archetypes - R2/R4][Ruby Leap Archetypes - R2/R4]]. To repeat:
- For {{{R1}}}/{{{R2}}}/{{{R4}}} leaps, leap just outside the minimum leap distance whenever you can.

Additionally:
- Spreads have a small end-window where you can leap without taking damage.

Additionally,
- {{{def}}} \rightarrow {{{secl}}} does provide back-to-back invuln, but (2/3) {{{sec}}} can roll {{{Shackles}}}.
- {{{secl}}} \rightarrow {{{secl}}} / {{{def}}} only chain invulns if <0.7s GCD or {{{Greysteel-Shield}}}.

** REVIEW Hidden Leap Disable Radius
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h:leap_buffer
:END:

After enabling leap on Secondary, disabling leap uses a smaller "buffer" distance.

#+begin_quote
When your leap is disabled, for most enemies[fn:small_enemy:Excludes small enemies like butterflies, dummy and mice spawns in Matti.]:
- Your Primary will always hit the enemy.
- Your Secondary[fn:sec_size:{{{sec}}} rectangle hitbox length is ~5% shorter than {{{pri}}}'s diameter] will always hit the enemy horizontally.

Due to this, I typically play with my leap disabled and move (just) outside the leap distance when needed.
#+end_quote
